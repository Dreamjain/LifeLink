# LifeLink – Vision Document

## Project Vision

LifeLink is a Smart Ambulance Response System designed to reduce the time taken to provide emergency medical assistance. The system aims to connect patients, hospitals, ambulance drivers, and administrators through a single platform that enables faster communication and coordination during medical emergencies.

## Problem Statement

During emergencies, patients often face delays in getting medical assistance due to:

* Difficulty contacting nearby hospitals.
* Lack of information about ambulance availability.
* Delayed communication with family members.
* Hospitals being unaware of the patient's condition before arrival.
* Manual coordination between hospitals, ambulance drivers, and patients.

These delays can significantly affect patient outcomes, especially in critical cases such as heart attacks, strokes, or severe accidents.

## Proposed Solution

LifeLink provides a one-touch emergency platform where a patient can request an ambulance using a single SOS button. Once an emergency request is created, the system automatically notifies the nearest suitable hospital, allows the hospital to assign an available ambulance, alerts the ambulance driver, and informs the patient's emergency contacts. Throughout the process, the ambulance and emergency status are tracked in real time.

## Primary Users

* Patient
* Hospital Staff
* Ambulance Driver
* Administrator

## Objectives

* Reduce emergency response time.
* Improve communication between patients and hospitals.
* Provide real-time ambulance tracking.
* Notify emergency contacts instantly.
* Allow hospitals to prepare before the patient arrives.
* Build a scalable platform that can be expanded with AI-based emergency prioritization and smart hospital integration in the future.

## Scope of the MVP

The first version of LifeLink will demonstrate:

* Patient registration and medical profile management.
* Hospital dashboard for accepting emergency requests.
* Ambulance driver dashboard.
* Real-time emergency workflow.
* Live ambulance tracking.
* Emergency contact notifications (simulated).
* Administrative management of hospitals and users.

## Future Scope

Future versions may include:

* AI-based emergency severity prediction.
* Wearable device integration.
* Integration with government emergency services.
* Hospital resource optimization.
* Digital health record integration.
